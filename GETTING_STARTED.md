## 🔑 Key Features

### 💊 Pharmacy Module
```
Upload medicine photo
         ↓
AI extracts text (OCR)
         ↓
Matches to database (NLP)
         ↓
Shows: Name, composition, dosage, warnings, 
       side effects, interactions, storage tips
         ↓
Save to history & track statistics
```

### 🔬 Dermatology Module
```
Upload skin photo
         ↓
AI analyzes visual features (CV)
         ↓
Matches to disease database
         ↓
Shows: Disease name, causes, symptoms,
       treatments, prevention, severity
         ↓
Save to history & track statistics
```
